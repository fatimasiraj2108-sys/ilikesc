Check your eligibility

Before you apply, it helps to understand how eligibility is decided. This page explains the full picture — not just marks and income — and gives you an estimated percentage chance based on six factors we actually weigh.

Scholarships are merit-based, but "merit" here means more than a single exam score. We weigh your academic performance alongside your household's real financial situation — because two students with the same marks can be in very different circumstances. These are guidelines, not hard cut-offs: every full application is still read by a reviewer, who can account for anything the numbers don't capture.

What counts as "family hardship"?

Things like a guardian losing their job, a serious illness or ongoing medical costs in the family, a recent death of a working family member, or any situation that's made this year financially harder than usual.

What if my percentage is low?

This is a rough estimate to help you plan, not a decision. Reviewers weigh your full circumstances, including anything you explain in the application essay. Apply anyway.
