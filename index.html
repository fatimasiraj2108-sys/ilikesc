Why this portal exists

Across Pakistan, thousands of students from low-income families are academically capable but financially unable to continue school. Their potential goes unrealised not because of ability, but because of cost — tuition, exam fees, books, and transport add up to more than many households make in a month. This portal exists to close that gap.

What we actually do

We find students with real academic potential and genuine financial need, walk them through a straightforward application, and connect approved students with scholarship funding that covers tuition — so a lack of money never has to be the reason a capable student's education stops.

Who this is for

Students in Grade 9 through A-Levels, currently studying in Karachi, Lahore, or Islamabad, whose household income makes it genuinely difficult to continue paying school or college fees — regardless of which school system or board they're in.

How it works

Four steps, from checking your eligibility to actually starting the next school year funded.

1

Check eligibility

Answer a few questions about your grade level, marks, household income, and circumstances to get an estimated chance for each track.

2

Apply

Fill in one complete application form and share Google Drive links to your mark sheet, income proof, and ID — no attachments needed.

3

Review

A reviewer reads your full application, not just the numbers. You can check your status anytime using your Application ID.

4

Funding & admission

If approved, we contact your guardian directly, confirm details, and coordinate funding with your school or college.
