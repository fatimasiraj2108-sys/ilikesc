Lower-cost schooling options to know about

If tuition is the barrier right now — before or alongside applying for a scholarship — a few general categories are worth knowing about. Costs and admission rules change often and vary by campus, so always confirm current fees directly with the school rather than relying on any number online, including ours.

Government schools & colleges

Free or very low-cost, run by the provincial education department. Visit your nearest government school office with a birth certificate or B-form to ask about admission.

Low-fee private schools

Many neighbourhoods have private schools with modest monthly fees and merit-based fee waivers. Ask directly about their fee structure and whether they offer need-based concessions.

Distance & open learning

Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU) offers matric, intermediate, and degree programmes at low cost with flexible study, a solid option if commuting or full-time attendance isn't possible.

Scholarships at your current school

Before switching schools, ask your current school's administration if they offer merit or need-based fee concessions — many do, and it can be simpler than transferring.
